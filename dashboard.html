How it came together

Built from the books the company already had.

Before

The firm’s revenue tracker, projections model, accounting system, corporate card and brokerage account each told a slightly different story. Answering “how much cash will we have in December?” meant an afternoon of spreadsheets, and nobody fully trusted the answer.

What we built

  • One overview with year-to-date results, the full-year outlook, open receivables, cash and a short list of what needs attention.
  • Project-level revenue showing completed, ongoing and upcoming work against the plan, plus anything finished but not yet invoiced.
  • Tie-outs that check the tracker, the model and the accounting system against each other, line by line.
  • An assistant that answers questions using the dashboard’s own data.

What changed

  • After reconciling the model, the mid-year cash forecast landed within 1.5% of actual.
  • Finished-but-unbilled work now surfaces the week it’s done, not at year end.
  • Sales tax returns are checked against how payments were booked, so mismatches get caught before the accountant finds them.
  • Leadership checks one page instead of asking for a report.
