Operations & Finance
Associate.

We run the back office for a set of US- and India-based startups, growing companies, and nonprofits. We're looking for someone in Mumbai to work closely with the founder across our clients and our own operations: an early-career role for a detail-driven person we can invest in and grow with.

Location
Mumbai · Flexible
Type
Full-time
Compensation
From ₹50,000/mo
Level
Early career

The role

This is a broad, hands-on job. On any given day you might reconcile a client's books, chase down an invoice, sort out a payroll question, help close the month, or keep a project moving. You'll work directly with the founder across our client base and our internal operations, with real exposure to how a small, fast-moving firm actually runs.

It suits someone who likes owning the details, works quickly, and wants to take on more responsibility over time. We'd rather find the right person early and grow the role around them than hire for a fixed job description.

What you'll do

  • Day-to-day accounting: reconciliation, AP/AR, expense tracking, and month-end close across our clients.
  • Client operations support: working across our full client base (startups, LLPs, LLCs, and nonprofits) on their finance and operations needs.
  • Keeping things running: vendor coordination, filings, records, and the various loose ends of a small firm.
  • Founder support: some personal accounting and travel and logistics coordination.

Who you are

  • Early in your career and eager to learn: someone we can invest in and grow with.
  • Some accounting or bookkeeping experience: QuickBooks is a plus. A CA, CA-Inter, or CA in progress is welcome but not required.
  • Detail-oriented: comfortable with numbers, and you catch what others miss.
  • Organized and reliable, with strong follow-through.
  • Good written English: most of our clients are US-based.
  • Responsive and reachable: comfortable with the pace of a small firm where priorities shift and things move quickly.
  • A hustler: you take ownership, figure things out, and don't wait to be told.

Compensation & growth

₹50,000 per month to start, adjusted for experience, with clear room to grow as you take on more. This is meant to be a role you expand from. You'll learn the finance and operations side of a real consulting business directly from the founder, working with US and India clients from day one.

Think this is you?

Send a short note about yourself and why this role fits, along with your CV. No need for anything formal. Tell us what you've worked on and what you're looking for.
